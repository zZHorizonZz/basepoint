AND keeps the rows that match both.
filter orders: state = "OPEN" AND total > 100
rows: 1, 5

OR keeps the rows that match either.
filter orders: total >= 220 OR region = US
rows: 2, 3, 5

Terms without an operator between them must all match.
filter orders: state = OPEN region = US
rows: 2, 5

NOT negates.
filter orders: NOT state = OPEN
rows: 3, 4

A minus negates.
filter orders: -state = OPEN
rows: 3, 4

OR binds before AND, as AIP-160 says.
filter orders: state = CLOSED AND region = EU OR total < 100
rows: 3

Parentheses group.
filter orders: (region = EU OR region = US) AND total < 200
rows: 1, 2

Parentheses nest.
filter orders: ((((((((((state = OPEN))))))))))
rows: 1, 2, 5

Empty text matches every row.
filter orders:
rows: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

A negation of a negation.
filter orders: NOT (NOT state = OPEN)
rows: 1, 2, 5
